Carrier: Autopilot Waypoints 0.1.0-client-beta.1

- Carrier waypoints on the aviation-control map: drag the ship or route points.
- Drop a route point onto another point to repeat the route.
- Animated helm/throttle and continuous forward power through turns.
- NAV panel: extend/clear route, start/pause, native engine power and defence.
- FULL is 100% game-native forward output, with no fixed knot ceiling.
- Optional radar-or-visual threat-triggered AA/CIWS/torpedo defensive arming.
- Native saved routes and manual helm/throttle takeover.
- Original Revolution command permissions and aircraft controls preserved.
- Manual DLL updates require copying the new DLL again; compatible CC2 ModLoader recommended.
- Requires CC2X SDK 0.10.0-dev. Local single-player client beta.
- Minimal Steam payload; full owned source, tests and tools on GitHub.
